Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Mirrorly does not collect or transmit any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no Mirrorly server. Everything it stores is kept by Firefox, on your own computer.

What Mirrorly stores

In Firefox's local extension storage, on this computer only: your settings. That's which devices and browsers you chose, which kinds of syncing are on, touch, user agent and keyboard options, text size and appearance, and whether the settings panel is open. It doesn't store the pages you visit.

Network requests

Mirrorly has no server and sends nothing anywhere. The requests it causes are all for the page you're already viewing:

  • The phone view loads the page. The sidebar shows the same address as your tab, so the site is loaded a second time, just as if you'd opened it in another tab.
  • Stylesheets, re-read. With Touch on (to switch off hover effects), or for sites that use iOS's text size or safe areas, Mirrorly re-reads the page's own stylesheets from the servers the page loaded them from.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Access your data for all websites: to show any site in the phone view, and to run the code that keeps the phone and the desktop tab in sync.
  • webRequest: most sites forbid being shown inside a frame. Mirrorly removes that rule for the phone view only, and sends the phone's user agent for its requests. Your normal tabs are untouched.
  • Tabs and navigation: to follow the tab you're on, its address, and reloads, so the phone shows the same page.
  • Downloads: to save screenshots to your Downloads folder.
  • Storage: for your settings.

Data shared with others

None. Mirrorly doesn't sell, share or transfer any data, because it doesn't collect any.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the change will be in the project's history on GitHub.

Contact
